# Program B — Creator program (accrual, no payouts)

Creator applications, admin approval, code issuance, attribution, and a full
commission ledger with risk scoring and a maturation state machine.

**No money leaves the building in this bundle.** Commissions accrue and mature
to `payable` and stop there. Payout identity collection, KYC and the payout
runner are deliberately the next step, because that is where tax and ops
obligations begin and they deserve their own review.

Branch: `feat/prompt-kit-script-variants` · Depends on Program A being applied
first (it cross-checks `referral_attributions`).

## The design in one paragraph

**A promoter signs up for KOKAO first.** From inside the product they apply with
their channels and accept the agreement. An admin approves, which issues a
`KC-XXXXXXXX` code. Another workspace enters that code — it *attaches*, granting
nothing. When that workspace later buys credits, the buyer gets bonus credits
immediately (they paid, they get their bonus) and the promoter accrues a cash
commission as `pending`. It matures to `payable` only when the refund window has
closed **and** the workspace has burned at least 25% of what it bought.
High-risk commissions land in `held` for manual review instead.

### Why promoter-must-sign-up is the right call

`creator_accounts.tenantId` is `NOT NULL` and uniquely indexed — one promoter
account per workspace. Three things fall out of that:

- **No separate creator auth to build.** Clerk session → tenant → promoter
  account. The application form is just another signed-in page.
- **"Can't use your own code" becomes enforceable** rather than aspirational —
  the promoter has a workspace id to compare against.
- **A better fraud signal.** Both sides are now KOKAO workspaces, so their
  signup times are comparable. Two accounts minted within an hour of each other
  is the classic shape of one person running both sides — that's a 30-point
  signal, added in this revision.

It also means every promoter has at least opened the product they're promoting,
which is worth something on its own.

## Why the consumption gate matters more than identity checks

You can't collect PAN at purchase. You don't need to.

A self-referrer buying through their own code at 10% is ₹9,000 down on a ₹10,000
purchase — cash out can never exceed cash in, so the worst case is a discount,
not extraction. What the consumption gate adds is that the commission never even
*matures* until the referred workspace burns real credits, which costs real
provider spend. Fraud has to genuinely lose money to unlock a fraction of it
back. A real customer crosses the threshold without noticing.

Identity resolves later, at payout, where PAN and bank account are available and
can be deduplicated and cross-checked against payment instruments. That's step 5.

## Risk signals implemented

Scored at accrual, stored on the commission row as `riskScore` + `riskSignals`:

| Signal | Weight |
|---|---|
| Creator and buyer share a non-freemail email domain | 35 |
| Email local parts match after stripping `+tag` and dots | 40 |
| Workspace under 30 minutes old at purchase | 20 |
| Creator's first N commissions | 25 |
| 10+ attaches on this creator's codes in 24h | 20 |
| Code attached at the moment of signup | 10 |
| Promoter and buyer workspaces created under an hour apart | 30 |

Anything at or above `riskHoldThreshold` lands in `held` rather than `pending`.
**The purchase is never blocked** — the buyer always gets their credits and
their bonus. Only the creator's money waits.

### The signal that isn't here yet

The strongest one — the same UPI VPA or card fingerprint paying in and receiving
out — needs the payment instrument, which only the gateway webhook sees.
`recordInvoice` doesn't carry it.

To add it: capture `payment.vpa` / `payment.card.last4` + issuer from the
Razorpay and Cashfree webhook payloads, hash them, store per tenant, and extend
`assessRisk` with a lookup. The function signature already takes an open
signals object, so it's an additive change. This is worth doing *before* the
first payout run, not before launch — it only matters when money can leave.

## Deliberate omissions

- **Payout runner, PAN/bank collection, KYC** — step 5. Tables exist, nothing writes.
- **Creator dashboard UI** — step 4. `getCreatorEarnings` returns everything it needs, including the "why is this pending" breakdown (`awaitingActivation` vs `inHoldWindow`). Show that; creators tolerate delay they can see.
- **Reserve offset on a post-payout refund.** `reverseCreatorCommission` reverses cleanly from pending/held/payable and **refuses** for `in_payout`/`paid`, logging a warning instead of pretending. Handling that properly means offsetting against the reserve and then a negative balance — it belongs with the payout runner.
- **Refund hook.** Nothing calls `reverseCreatorCommission` yet. Wire it into your refund/chargeback path when you have one.
