# Patch 10 — noise floor under lip-sync audio

Applies to `feat/prompt-kit-script-variants`, after patches 8 and 9.

```bash
git am 0010-*.patch
```

No schema change, no new dependencies.

## What it does

The gaps between sentences in a narration track come from `silence()`, which is
`Buffer.alloc` — every sample exactly zero. True digital silence does not occur
in the recordings lip-sync models are trained on.

On your generated-character render the model's behaviour through those gaps was
degenerate: instead of closing the lips it held the mouth half-open with a black
void where the teeth should be, while articulating cleanly the moment speech
resumed. Four of that clip's seven pauses measured above its own mean mouth
aperture.

This lays uniform noise at a peak of -60 dBFS — about 40dB under ordinary
speech — under every slice handed to the model, so its input sits back inside
the distribution it has seen.

## Two things worth knowing

**It is a hypothesis, not a proven fix.** One observation supports it. It is
built to be cheap to undo: delete the `applyNoiseFloor` call inside
`sliceNarration` and the model receives exactly what it received before.

**The audio you hear is untouched.** The floor is scoped to `sliceNarration`,
whose only caller is the lip-sync path. That matters — the compositor runs
`loudnorm` over the narration track twice, and a floor added there could be
amplified into audible hiss.

## Verified

Applied with `git am` to a fresh clone of the branch on top of 8 and 9;
api-server typecheck clean; videoGen suite **790 passed**, against the same two
pre-existing failures documented in INSTALL-8-9.md.

Two of my own earlier tests asserted bit-exact sample values and now assert
provenance within tolerance. That is the intended consequence — the point of
the patch is that nothing is bit-identical any more — not a workaround.

## How to tell whether it worked

Render the same character story again and run:

```bash
node scripts/src/lipsync-inspect.mjs <downloaded.mp4>
```

Look at the pause sheets. Pass is lips together. The current failure looks
unmistakable once you have seen it: a half-open mouth with a black gap where
the teeth belong, frozen across every frame of the pause.

If it still fails, the next thing I would try is a short lead-in and lead-out of
speech-adjacent audio around each slice rather than a flat floor — but try this
first, it is twenty lines.

## Unrelated, but visible in that render

Every shot in the sample was the same extreme close-up. Your scene planner asks
for "wide establishing frames, medium shots, and intimate close-ups" and is
getting none of that variety. That is a prompt problem rather than a sync one,
and it is also why the mouth artifact is so prominent — a medium shot would both
hide it and give you real coverage.
