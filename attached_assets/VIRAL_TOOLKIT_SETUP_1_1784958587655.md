# Patch 3 of 5 — Viral toolkit

One idea in, platform-ready marketing out.

## Apply (on top of music-suite.patch)

```bash
git am viral-toolkit.patch
```

No schema change, no new dependencies, no new secrets. Restart the app.

## What you get

**Hook ideas** (video studio, next to the topic input) — five ways to open the video, each a different proven pattern: question, bold claim, contrarian, curiosity gap, stat, story. Pick one and the script opens with it. Free, brand-voice-aware.

**Start from a template** (video studio) — 8 proven short-form formats (listicle, myth-busting, before/after, insider secrets…) that pre-fill the topic; drop your subject into the blank.

**Platform pack** (caption studio, below the Caption/Image buttons) — one brief becomes a natively-written caption + hashtags + CTA for Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, X, and Threads, each respecting its platform's rules (X fits 280 characters including tags; LinkedIn keeps it to 3 hashtags; Threads reads like a text to a friend). Costs exactly one caption (quota first, then a credit — refunded if generation fails). Copy per platform with one click. Brand-kit voice rules and your taste memory apply, same as regular captions.
