# Patch 1 of 5 — Dynamic word-timed captions

The short-form caption style: big 2–3 word groups popping in sync with the narration, instead of a full sentence sitting at the bottom. This is the single most visible quality jump left from the study (A1).

## Apply (on top of reliability-core.patch)

```bash
git am captions.patch
```

No schema change, no new dependencies, no new secrets. Restart the app.

## What you get

- **New "Caption style" picker** under the Subtitles switch in Topic to Video: **Dynamic** (default for new videos) or **Classic** (the previous sentence style).
- Dynamic captions are bigger, heavier-stroked, and sit at the short-form focal point of the frame. Works in every language — CJK scripts group by characters instead of spaces.
- **Zero extra AI cost**: word timing is computed from the narration's exact per-sentence measurements (no transcription model needed). Deterministic — same script, same timing, every render.
- Full contract support (`captionStyle` on the generate-video API), so mobile can offer the same toggle.

## Sequence note

Apply order matters: this patch expects reliability-core.patch to be applied first (it builds on the same composition code). All five patches in this series are strictly sequential.
