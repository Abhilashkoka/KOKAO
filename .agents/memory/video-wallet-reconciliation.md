---
name: Video wallet reconciliation
description: Durable rules for exact multi-operation video billing and safe historical corrections.
---

Successful retry-chain video billing is the sum of every durable provider cost attributable to the delivered workflow: script generation, character portraits/reference sheets, outfits, backdrops, narration, visuals, video scenes, music, B-roll, lip-sync, localization, and any other paid input actually used in the delivery. Apply the platform fee once to the complete raw total and round once. Read the dynamic fee when the immutable delivery manifest is created and freeze it there; never hardcode or re-read it during later reconciliation. Provider events need stable identities that survive copied checkpoints, and chain roots must be resolved transitively because legacy retries may point to an intermediate parent. Missing event prices keep the chain pending; never use a flat estimate.

**Why:** The admin controls the platform fee and may change it from 20%; a fixed value silently misprices new work. Aggregate fallback estimates also undercharge mixed-model and failover jobs whenever one provider event is unpriced. Historical correction can race a queued original settlement and permanently produce the wrong total if it runs before that settlement becomes immutable.

**How to apply:** Freeze exact receipts and the fee before exposing success. A separately settled input contributes its raw cost and prior payment to the first delivery that claims its stable identity; later deliveries reuse it at zero incremental cost and cannot re-credit the same payment. Link each narration cue to the video job before provider dispatch so partial failures remain recoverable. Keep aggregate true-ups manifest-owned and unanchored to shared component reservations. Conflicting or incomplete evidence stays pending. Failed, discarded, duplicated, unmapped, and unusable work is excluded unless its artifact is reused. Historical reports stay read-only until complete evidence and separate approval permit correction.
