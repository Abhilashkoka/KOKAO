description: Source and overwrite boundaries for automatic video titles, captions, and hashtags.
---
Generate social copy from persisted spoken scripts first; explicitly label original-brief fallback rather than implying visual analysis.

**Why:** video generation prompts can differ from the final spoken story, and a source-less upload cannot reliably support factual copy about its footage.

**How to apply:** preserve tenant ownership when resolving a Library video's source. Keep funded caption generation separate from saving content; review remains editable and asynchronous suggestions must not overwrite manual changes. Reusing an in-flight or already generated suggestion should not create another paid call.
