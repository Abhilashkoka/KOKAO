---
name: App Walkthrough recording timing
description: Preserve full uploaded screen recordings through composition, not just clip fitting.
---
Uploaded walkthrough recordings must remain complete even when their voiceover is shorter. Expand the visual timeline and move subsequent spoken cues with it; padding the input clip alone is insufficient.

**Why:** A media encoder can preserve every frame while the final composer still cuts it to the narration duration. This would silently remove software-demo steps.

**How to apply:** For changes to walkthrough planning, storyboard edits, retries, or composition, verify final output duration and closing-line placement as well as the standalone fitted clip. Character picture-in-picture and cursor zoom are outside the supplied template's intended behavior.
