---
name: Creative direction briefs
description: Durable rules for resolving and consuming video-template creative intent.
---

Creative direction is typed, bounded guidance resolved in one deterministic pass: format, template, vertical, brand, then user. Merge lists by normalized union with caps, intersect ranges, report clamps, and reject required/forbidden vocabulary conflicts.

**Why:** Re-reading templates or brand state during retries makes identical jobs render differently, while letting broad style text replace a scene subject can change what the user asked to depict.

**How to apply:** Snapshot the resolved brief and field provenance when the job is enqueued; retries and resumed storyboards consume that snapshot. Compile narrow fragments for each generator, keep the user topic separate from scene subjects, and never allow template guidance to carry tenant assets or identifiers.
