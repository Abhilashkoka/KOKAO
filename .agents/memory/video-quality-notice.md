---
name: Video quality notice
description: User-required expectation setting for AI video generation.
---

Whenever a video is generated, tell the user that generation may not be perfect every time and there could be glitches. Show the notice before generation and with completed videos on web and mobile; encourage review before sharing or publishing.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested this to set expectations about imperfect AI output.

**How to apply:** Keep the notice visible across video modes and new completion surfaces. It is an informational notice, not a required consent checkbox or a guarantee of quality.
